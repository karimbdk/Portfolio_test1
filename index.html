About Me

Designer, Thinker, Creator

Bringing brands to life through thoughtful design and creative vision

I'm Kanakala Sai Rahul, a self-taught graphic designer with a passion for creating meaningful visual experiences. Graduated from Osmania University, I've spent the last 5+ years honing my craft across logo design, brand identity, poster design, illustration, and digital media.

My design philosophy is rooted in simplicity and impact — I believe the best designs are those that communicate instantly while leaving a lasting impression. From minimal line art portraits to bold event posters, and from geometric logo marks to complete brand identity systems, I approach every project with the same dedication to quality and creativity.
